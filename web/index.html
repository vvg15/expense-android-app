Appearance

Set a monthly limit for the categories you want to keep an eye on. Leave the rest empty.

Tap a category to choose whether it counts as spending. Transfers and Investments don’t count by default. Removing a category keeps past expenses; they just show in grey.

Find payments in SMS…

Read bank, UPI and card SMS for the dates you choose. You review every expense before it’s added.

Import from SMS…

A .json backup restores everything (expenses, recurring payments, settings) and can be imported by any future version of this app. The .csv spreadsheet lists all expenses and recurring payments (see the “Type” column), opens in Excel or Google Sheets, and can be imported back.

Import from SMS

1. Load your messages

No SMS access? Save a copy of your messages first:

  1. Install the free app SMS Backup & Restore (by SyncTech) from the Play Store.
  2. Open it → Back up → turn on Messages only → choose Local backup (on your phone) → Back up now.
  3. Come back here and choose that .xml file.

The file is read on your phone only. It isn’t saved or sent anywhere.
