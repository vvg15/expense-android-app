# Privacy Policy — Expense Manager

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Expense Manager ("the app") is a personal expense tracker for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. In short: **everything stays on your phone.**

## What the app stores
The expenses, recurring payments, categories, budget and settings you enter or confirm. This data is stored **only on your device**, inside the app's private storage.

## SMS messages (optional)
If you choose to turn on *Add expenses from SMS*, the app asks for Android's **SMS permission (READ_SMS)** and uses it only to:

- read messages in your SMS inbox for the dates being checked,
- find payment messages from banks, cards and UPI services (for example "Rs 450 debited … to Zomato"),
- suggest them to you as expenses, which you review and confirm before anything is saved.

How SMS data is handled:

- Messages are **read and analysed on your device only**. They are **never uploaded, sold or shared** with anyone, including the developer.
- The app **has no internet permission**, so it cannot send data off your device.
- Messages from ordinary phone numbers are skipped by default, and OTPs and personal messages are ignored.
- **No message text is stored.** The app keeps only the expenses you confirm (amount, date, category, payment method, a short note such as the merchant name) and an internal identifier so the same message isn't suggested twice.
- The permission is **optional**; the rest of the app works without it. You can turn the feature off in the app's Settings, and remove the permission any time in Android Settings → Apps → Expense Manager → Permissions → SMS.

## App lock (optional)
If you turn on App lock, the app asks Android to verify you with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android performs the check. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint, face data, PIN or pattern.

## Automatic backups (optional)
If you choose a backup folder, the app saves backup files of your expense data into that folder on a schedule you set. The folder is chosen by you, and only that folder is accessed. If it's a cloud folder (e.g. Google Drive), that service's own terms apply to the files stored there.

## Files you choose
When you export a backup or spreadsheet, or import a file, the app only reads or writes the file you pick. Where that file goes (your phone, Google Drive, email, and so on) is your choice.

## Information we do not collect
The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting. The developer receives no data from the app.

## Updates via Google Play
If you installed the app from Google Play, Google Play may check for and install updates according to Google's own policies.

## Device backups
If Android's own backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, under Google's terms. You can turn this off in Android settings.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data
Use *Settings → Delete all data* in the app, or uninstall the app. Both permanently remove its data from your device.
